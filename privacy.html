Plain-language privacy information

This Policy applies when you browse weeboo.com, send a project request, communicate with us, become a Client, pay for services or receive support. It does not replace the privacy policy required for a Client’s own website.

01

Overview

Who is responsible for your information

Weeboo operates this website and determines how the personal information described in this Policy is used. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, Weeboo is the controller of that information unless this Policy or a project agreement says otherwise.

Questions and privacy requests may be sent to hello@weeboo.com or through our Contact page.

02

Information we collect

Information you give us and information generated by use

01

Identity & contact

Name, email address, organisation, role and other contact details you provide.

02

Project information

Your idea, goals, audience, service needs, timeline, materials, existing website and instructions.

03

Communications

Emails, feedback, approvals, support messages, meeting notes and other correspondence.

04

Commercial records

Proposals, contracts, invoices, payment status and transaction references. We do not need to store full card details.

05

Website & technical data

IP address, browser, device, requested pages, timestamps, security events and server logs created when the site is delivered.

06

Admin & support data

Information needed to provide a private admin link, maintain a Client website, diagnose issues and protect access.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless we ask for it and it is necessary for the project. If a project will involve special-category, regulated or high-risk data, we will discuss appropriate requirements separately.

03

How we use information

Specific and practical purposes

  • receive, review and respond to project requests;
  • clarify requirements and prepare proposals;
  • plan, write, design, develop, test and launch websites;
  • provide hosting, maintenance, admin access and support;
  • manage contracts, invoices, payments and business records;
  • authenticate access and protect websites, systems and users;
  • diagnose problems and improve service quality;
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory duties;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims; and
  • send service communications and, with a valid basis, relevant marketing that you can opt out of.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects unless we separately explain the process and provide the rights required by law.

04

Legal bases

Why processing is permitted

Where a law such as the GDPR requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following:

ContractTo take requested steps before a contract and to deliver agreed services.
Legitimate interestsTo operate, secure and improve our business, communicate with Clients, prevent misuse and pursue lawful claims, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
Legal obligationTo keep required records and respond to lawful authorities or legal process.
ConsentWhere we specifically ask for permission, including certain marketing or optional technologies. Consent may be withdrawn.
05

Sharing

When another organisation may receive information

We share personal information only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and contractual controls. Recipients may include:

  • hosting, infrastructure, storage and security providers;
  • email, communication and project-support providers;
  • domain registrars, integration providers and technical vendors chosen for a project;
  • payment and invoicing providers, which may process payment details under their own privacy notices;
  • professional advisers such as accountants, insurers and lawyers;
  • authorities or other parties where disclosure is legally required or necessary to protect rights and security; and
  • a buyer, investor or successor involved in a genuine business transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We may also share information at your direction, for example when you ask us to connect a third-party service to your website.

06

International transfers

Services may operate across borders

Weeboo, its team and service providers may process information in countries different from yours. Those countries may have different privacy laws. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, we use an approved mechanism such as an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another lawful basis, together with additional protections where appropriate.

You may contact us for more information about safeguards relevant to your personal information.

07

Retention

We keep information only as long as needed

Retention depends on why the information was collected, the project relationship, security needs, dispute periods and legal, tax or accounting requirements. As a general framework:

  • unconverted project enquiries may be kept for up to 24 months so we can respond and understand prior discussions;
  • Client, contract, invoice and project records may be kept for the relationship and the legally required recordkeeping or claims period afterward;
  • support and technical records are kept for the period reasonably needed to provide service, diagnose issues and protect security; and
  • marketing preference records are kept until you opt out, plus a minimal suppression record so we can respect that choice.

We may delete, anonymise or aggregate information when it is no longer required. Backup copies may remain for a limited period until they are overwritten in the normal cycle.

08

Security

Administrative, technical and organisational safeguards

We use safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information, including access limitation, authentication, secure service providers, maintenance practices, backups where included, and procedures for responding to suspected incidents. Access is limited to people and providers who need it for their work.

No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You must protect your devices, email account, private admin link and credentials, and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

09

Your choices & rights

You can ask how your information is handled

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to:

Access your information Correct inaccurate information Request deletion Restrict processing Object to certain uses Receive portable data Withdraw consent Complain to a regulator

Rights are not absolute and lawful exemptions may apply. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. You will not be discriminated against for exercising a privacy right.

To opt out of marketing, use the unsubscribe method in the message or contact us. Service and transactional messages may still be sent when needed for a project or subscription.

10

Cookies & technology

How the website is delivered

Our hosting infrastructure may create standard server logs and use essential technical storage needed to deliver pages, secure the service or remember an action you request. External asset providers, such as a web-font provider, may receive technical request information including an IP address and browser details when their files are loaded.

We will not use non-essential analytics or advertising cookies without providing any notice and choice required by applicable law. If our use of these technologies changes materially, we will update this Policy and, where required, provide cookie controls.

11

Client websites

Privacy roles depend on the website

A website we build for a Client is operated for that Client’s own purposes. The Client normally decides why and how visitor, customer, employee or user information on that website is used and is responsible for its own privacy policy, notices, lawful bases and consent controls.

Where Weeboo processes personal information only to host, maintain or support a Client website on the Client’s documented instructions, Weeboo may act as a processor or service provider. Additional data-processing terms may apply where required. This Policy covers Weeboo’s own use of information; it does not cover a Client’s independent use of data on its website.

Children

Weeboo’s services are intended for adults and organisations, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child who cannot lawfully provide it. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information improperly.

Third-party links

Our website and Client projects may link to services operated by others. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices, and Weeboo is not responsible for them.

12

Updates & contact

Privacy questions or requests

We may update this Policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how we use information already collected, we will provide additional notice where required.

Contact Weeboo at hello@weeboo.com or use our Contact page. If applicable law gives you the right to lodge a complaint, you may contact the data-protection authority for your location. We encourage you to contact us first so we can try to resolve the concern.
