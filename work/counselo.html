The project

One platform.
Different legal contexts.

The challenge

CounselO needed to make a large and sensitive body of legal information understandable across three jurisdictions and two languages. Visitors needed a clear regional starting point, meaningful service paths and a direct route to consultation without treating every legal role or rule as identical.

Our response

We shaped a jurisdiction-first architecture supported by shared bilingual components, regional service and problem pages, clear consultation routes and a publishing system designed to keep a large content inventory consistent.

What Weeboo delivered

More than a visual redesign.
A complete content system.
